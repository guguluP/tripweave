# TripWeave

Three honest Puri hotels. All-in rupee prices. A Trust Score you can read.

Sign in with Google, X, or email, then hold a stay with card, UPI, or net banking. Checkout is a sandbox processor — we never store the full card number, UPI secret, or PIN.

## Try it

1. Sign in or create an account
2. Answer the brief and pick one of three stays
3. Pay with `4242 4242 4242 4242`, `you@okaxis`, or net-banking PIN `123456`

Declines: card `4000 0000 0000 0002`, UPI `fail@okaxis`, PIN `000000`.
