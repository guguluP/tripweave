# Group trip breach playbook

Applies to name and phone data collected when someone joins a group trip.

1. Confirm the event is a disclosure of member names, phone numbers, or payment references, not a public shortlist.
2. Stop the leak: rotate the share token, revoke the affected desk or service credential, and block the route if it is still returning phones.
3. Record what was exposed, when it started, and which trips were in the window.
4. Tell the board contact and the people whose numbers were exposed. The DPDP notice window is part of the duties that apply from 13 May 2027; do not wait for that date to tell affected members.
5. Delete the exposed phone values if the trip is over, and confirm group tables stay closed to the anon key.
6. Write down the cause and the fix. Do not store UPI VPAs, full payment instrument numbers, or child names in the follow-up.
